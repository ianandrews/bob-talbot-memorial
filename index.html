In Loving Memory

Robert J. "Bob" Talbot

1938 – October 2026

Catholic University '59 · Basketball & Baseball standout · Head Baseball Coach 1965–77 · Athletics Director · Dean of Admissions and Financial Aid

Bob Talbot, 88, was a class of 1959 graduate of The Catholic University of America and a standout athlete in men's basketball and baseball. He served as head baseball coach from 1965 to 1977 and as athletics director for over a decade. In 2000 the baseball program dedicated its field as Robert J. Talbot Field. He was inducted into the Catholic Athletics Hall of Fame on June 8, 1985. Away from athletics he served as Dean of Admissions and Financial Aid. As a student-athlete he scored 1,187 points in basketball (24th all-time) and shares the baseball program record with 17 strikeouts in a single game.
