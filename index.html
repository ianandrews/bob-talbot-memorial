In Loving Memory

Robert J. "Bob" Talbot

November 14, 1937 – October 5, 2026

Catholic University '59 · Basketball & Baseball standout · Head Baseball Coach 1965–77 · Athletics Director · Dean of Admissions and Financial Aid

Robert J. “Bob” Talbot was born November 14, 1937, in Washington, DC, and died October 5, 2026, at home in Chevy Chase, Maryland, at age 88. He starred in baseball and basketball at St. John's College High School and The Catholic University of America (class of 1959), earning induction into both schools' athletic halls of fame — Catholic's on June 8, 1985. He returned to Catholic University in 1963 and spent four decades there: as head baseball coach from 1965 to 1977, whose 1977 squad fell just short of the College World Series; two decades in Admissions and Financial Aid, ultimately as Dean; and a final decade as Athletics Director. In 2000 the baseball program dedicated its field as Robert J. Talbot Field. As a student-athlete he scored 1,187 points in basketball (24th all-time) and shares the baseball program's single-game record of 17 strikeouts. After concluding his Catholic University career in 2004, he spent eight years with the Yellow Ribbon Fund, mentoring nearly 800 wounded service members at Walter Reed and Fort Belvoir. He married Barbara Gilvey on December 17, 1966; they shared nearly sixty years together. He is survived by Barbara, their children Greg, Mark, Megan, and Sara, and ten grandchildren.
